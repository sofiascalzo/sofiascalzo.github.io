

# A word on this space

Content here is sparse by design.

I only write about things that genuinely required me to think —
challenges where current models weren't enough,
where the answer wasn't a Google search away,
where I had to sit with the problem until something clicked.

This blog is more for me than for the views.
If it helps you too, that's a bonus.

# A word on AI

Yes, I use AI tools. I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

But let's be honest about what they're actually good for:
boilerplate, syntax, rubber ducking at 2am.
The moment a challenge requires real reasoning,
chaining vulnerabilities, finding the weird edge case,
understanding *why* something breaks instead of just *that* it breaks,
current models fall apart in interesting ways.

That gap is where the fun lives.
That gap is also why everything in this blog exists.

To everyone saying CTFs are dying because of AI:
you're wrong, and also kind of right.

AI didn't kill the difficulty,  it **raised the floor**.
The boring parts got automated.
What's left is everything that required a human all along.

*We didn't lose a game. We unlocked a new difficulty tier.*
