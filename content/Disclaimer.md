# A word on AI & CTFs

I use AI tools. I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

But let's be honest about what they're actually good for:
boilerplate, syntax, rubber ducking at 2am.

That gap is where the fun lives.

---

To everyone saying CTFs are dying because of AI:

you're wrong, and also kind of right.

AI didn't kill the difficulty, it **raised the floor**.
The boring parts got automated.
What's left is everything that required a human all along.

*We didn't lose a game. We unlocked a new difficulty tier.*
